Still Need Help?

hello@lerntix.com

What makes Lerntix different from other LMS platforms?

Lerntix combines AI-driven learning paths, hands-on labs, and real-time progress analytics. Unlike traditional LMS, we offer adaptive quizzes, industry-verified certificates, and a vibrant mentor community — all in one intuitive dashboard.

Are the certificates recognized by employers?

Every certificate is co-signed by our industry partners and comes with a verifiable link you can add straight to your LinkedIn profile or résumé.

Can I try Lerntix before subscribing?

Yes. Start with a 7-day free trial that unlocks full access to every course, lab, and quiz — no card required to begin.

Are the courses self-paced or live?

Both. Most tracks are self-paced with lifetime access, while selected cohorts run weekly live sessions and Q & A with the instructor.

Is there a mobile app for offline learning?

Our iOS and Android apps let you download lessons, keep your progress in sync, and pick up right where you left off — even without a connection.

Can teams or businesses use Lerntix?

Lerntix for Business adds seat management, custom learning paths, and team analytics so you can upskill your whole organisation from one dashboard.
